How to play

Select
Tap a unit. Double-tap to grab every unit of that kind on screen. Hold your finger still for a moment, then drag, to draw a selection box (or turn on the box button at left).
Command
With units selected, tap a tree, mine or bush to gather, an enemy to attack, a foundation to build, or open ground to move.
Camera
Drag with one finger to pan. Pinch to zoom. Tap the minimap to jump.
Build
Select villagers, pick a building, tap the map where it should go, then press the check mark. For walls, tap the start, tap the end, then confirm.
Turtle tools
Palisades in the Dark Age, stone walls, gates and towers in Feudal, castles in Castle Age. Ring the town bell to pull villagers inside the town center, where each one adds an arrow.
Win
Destroy the rival's town centers and castles and all their villagers.
Desktop
Left-click or drag to select, right-click to command, wheel to zoom, WASD or arrows to pan.
